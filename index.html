Questions

Good to know.

Is root canal treatment painful?

With modern rotary instruments and effective anaesthesia, a root canal feels similar to getting a filling. Most treatments are completed in a single sitting.

Do you offer EMI for implants and aligners?

Yes. No-cost EMI is available on implants, aligners and smile makeovers through leading finance partners. Ask us at your consultation.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. Call us for severe toothache, swelling, broken teeth or injuries, and we'll do our best to see you the same day.

How much does a consultation cost?

A detailed consultation is ₹300 and is adjusted against your treatment if you proceed with us.

Is there parking near the clinic?

Yes, parking is available right outside the clinic. We're also easy to reach by metro and auto.
